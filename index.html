      The eye behind the camera

      I'm Nazih Talbi, a photographer and filmmaker from Algeria. I've worked freelance since 2022 on short films, commercials and social media content, and since 2024 I've been the photographer and videographer at Almaahed International.

      How a project runs

      1. We talk

        You tell me what you need, who it's for and when. A call or a message is enough.

      2. I plan

        Locations, timing, light and a shot list built around what matters most to you.

      3. We shoot

        Calm and discreet on the day. I direct only when it helps.

      4. You receive

        Edited photos and a graded film, ready to post, in the formats each platform needs.
